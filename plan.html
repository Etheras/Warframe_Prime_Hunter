How this works

How to crack them. One refinement per relic — you can't hold the same relic two ways. Each is set to clear its scarcest wanted reward fastest, not for the best overall hit rate: a common you'd pick up anyway shouldn't decide how you spend a relic you still need a rare from. The row colour is the refinement to take it to; the chips beneath are the parts you want, listed rarest first and coloured by rarity — gold, silver, bronze.

Crack them somewhere endless. A Void Fissure on an endless mission pays a free relic for staying — the fifth rotation gives a random Exceptional of the fissure's tier, the tenth a Flawless. The short missions have nothing to stay in, so the same stack of relics is worth more opened on a Survival or a Defense than on a Capture. The ranking on the left already accounts for this on any node carrying a fissure right now, and marks the row +free relic; this side of the page is where it matters when you are deciding what to open rather than where to go.
