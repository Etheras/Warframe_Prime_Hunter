How this works

How to crack them. One refinement per relic — you can't hold the same relic two ways. Each is set to clear its scarcest wanted reward fastest, not for the best overall hit rate: a common you'd pick up anyway shouldn't decide how you spend a relic you still need a rare from. The row colour is the refinement to take it to; the chips beneath are the parts you want, listed rarest first and coloured by rarity — gold, silver, bronze.
